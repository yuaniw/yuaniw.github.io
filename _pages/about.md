<h2 id="about-me"><span aria-hidden="true">👋</span> About</h2>

Hi! I’m Zhiyuan Guan (关致远), a second-year master’s student at Global College, [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/), supervised by [Prof. Yutong Ban](https://banyutong.github.io/sirius_lab_website/index.html).

I received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from [Wuhan University](https://www.whu.edu.cn/) in 2025.

My research topics include **dexterous manipulation**, **physical agents**, and **embodied AI**. Previously, I worked on recommender systems.

Feel free to [email me](mailto:benjaminguan@sjtu.edu.cn) if you’re interested in academic collaboration or have any questions. You can also reach me on WeChat.
